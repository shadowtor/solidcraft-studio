# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release. Models in SolidCraft through its MCP, then three finishes:

- Print: nozzle- and filament-aware fits, OrcaSlicer H2C check on six build plates, clean-support presets, optional second-filament interface (estimate).
- Render: headless and live Blender exploded views, Cycles glass, MP4 and GIF, EffectCraft effects.
- Textured: MaterialCraft maps into TextureCraft, UV helper, bake, paint, GLB export.

Not yet tried: a real print, Unreal and Unity import, EffectCraft's live-app bridge, `.mcmat` sharing.
